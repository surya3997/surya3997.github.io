App privacy policy

Updated 18 October 2026

This policy describes the restored-ad releases of Ad counter, BenTen guess, Flappy Flutter Dash, YATTT (Yet Another Tic Tac Toe), and Pixel Eden, published by Splender Creatorz.

Game data and accounts

These apps do not offer user accounts. Scores, progress and settings are stored on your device where the app supports saving them. You can remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Clearing data may permanently remove your saved progress.

Advertising data

The apps use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information including IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, crash and diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers such as the app set ID. Google uses these data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. This data collection is required when the ad service operates and is not limited to ephemeral processing. Data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

The restored-ad releases request non-personalized ads, apply child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment to all ad requests, restrict ad content to Google's G rating, and remove the Android advertising ID permission. These settings limit advertising data use but do not mean that no data is collected. The apps do not ask for precise location, contacts, photos or a user account to show ads.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for details about Google's processing.

Children and families

The apps use the protective ad-request settings described above for all users. We do not ask children to submit personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through our apps.

Deletion and controls

There is no in-app account or developer-operated data-deletion request feature. Device-local scores and progress can be cleared through Android settings. Google's privacy controls and retention practices apply to data processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy for available controls.
